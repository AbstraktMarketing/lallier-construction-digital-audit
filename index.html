Every one of the 44 cookies on lcidenver.com — including Google Analytics and ZoomInfo tracking cookies — fires before a visitor gives consent. A TermsFeed notice is present on the page, but it is display-only: it doesn't block cookies or recognize opt-out signals. Twelve states now legally require websites to detect and honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals automatically, before a user takes any action. These laws apply based on where the visitor is located, not where the company operates, so any U.S.-facing website is in scope. Colorado — Lallier's home market and one of the most active enforcers — is on that list. California has issued seven-figure fines for exactly this configuration, and Ford Motor Company was required in March 2026 to audit every tracking technology on its properties for GPC non-compliance.

!
GPC Compliance Gap — CA, CO, CT, DE, MD, MN, MT, NE, NH, NJ, OR, and TX require automatic GPC signal recognition. Visitors from these states currently receive no opt-out protection on this site. Verify the state count against the AMG compliance sheet before publishing.
